How to season your cast iron Tawa/ Kadai

by Santhosh Leelakrishnan 06 Oct 2025

Cooking on a cast-iron tawa isn’t just about tradition—it’s about taste, health, and durability. But to get the most out of your cast iron cookware, proper seasoning is key. A well-seasoned cast iron tawa develops a natural non-stick surface, enhances flavors, and prevents rusting.

In this guide, we’ll walk you through how to season your cast-iron tawa at home—the right way.

Also, let me tell you why seasoning is important-

  • It prevents tawa from rusting easily

  • Makes cooking easier with less sticking

  • Increases the life of your tawa

  • Adds flavor depth to food
