Season & Care
Season & Care
x

Pre-seasoned and ready to cook. A few minutes of care keeps it non-stick for generations.

1
First use

Warm the pan, sprinkle some salt and stir until it turns golden brown. Switch off, let it cool, wipe it clean with a cloth and rub on a thin coat of oil.

2
After every cook

Wash with a mild liquid soap and a soft scrub, coconut fibre or cloth. Dry it straight away and rub a few drops of oil so it is ready for next time.

3
Cooking

Metal and wooden spatulas are both fine — just don’t press hard. Tomato, tamarind and lemon are fine too; wash the pan soon after.

4
Re-season now and then

If food starts to stick or the surface looks dull, warm the pan, rub on a thin coat of oil and let it heat for a few minutes. Do this whenever it needs it.

Spotted rust? Don’t throw it away.

Scrub the rust off with steel wool or a metal scourer, wash with a little soap and water, dry it immediately and oil it lightly. Cast iron always comes back.
